# ADR-0007 — Phase 1 benchmarks the game's real sprite format

**Status:** accepted (2026-10-18, owner)

## Context

The original plan, `docs/05` and the placeholder profile assume 32×48 cells, 4 directions, an 8-frame walk and
chibi 3/4 top-down art. The DYL-70 survey of the Twos Are Up game measured something else
(`~/Projects/TwosAreUp` @ `ebf3272`; see `profiles/twos-are-up.yaml`):
- **Cells:** square cells set per character (120–288; 1.8 m humanoids are 136–184) at 62 px/m.
- **Directions:** 8, in the row order N, NW, W, SW, S, SE, E, NE. West-side animation frames are mirrored from east.
- **Clips:** idle, run, attack, cast, death and channel. There is **no walk and no hurt** clip.
- **Art:** one shared 255-colour palette, hard alpha, a 1 px near-black outline, and a sticker border drawn by the
  shader. Side-view standees are shown under a 3D orbit camera.
- **Origin:** every sheet was generated by PixelLab from the owner's OpenAI-made references.

A 4-direction 32×48 walk cannot ship into this game.

## Decision

1. **Target.** Phase 1 measures a usable **run cycle in the game's own format**:
   - Generate N, NE, E, SE and S; mirror the west side.
   - Use each character's own cell and frame count; the foot line sits at cell − 4.
   - Lock to the shipped palette.
   - The PLAN.md headline metric and the 60-minute cut-off apply to this run cycle wherever PLAN/docs say "4-dir walk".
2. **References.**
   - #1 `samurai`: one-handed katana.
   - #2 `oathguard`: kite shield.
   - #3 `relay_sentinel`: floating construct. It has no run, so its 4-frame hover idle stands in for locomotion.
     It is recorded, but does not decide the lead.
3. **Reference licensing.** The PixelLab sheets are approved for benchmark use only: model inputs, style references
   and ground truth. They are **not** approved as training data until the PixelLab ToS has been reviewed for that use.
4. **Tracks A/B (svntax).** They can only emit small 4-direction grids, so they run as a reduced floor baseline:
   - 3 characters × 2 seeds, plus × 2 checkpoints for B.
   - They are scored honestly and stay experimental and no-ship.
   - They can never be the production lead without licensing clearance.
5. **Registration.** Registration is per clip, as the game does it: the clip's contact frames sit on the foot line,
   and frames keep their vertical bob. Validator limits are calibrated on the shipped ground-truth clips before any
   track is scored.
6. **Storage.** This repo is public and the game is private, so no game art, palette values or generated images of
   these characters are committed here. Inputs and outputs live in `~/sonoranlab-work/benchmark/`. The repo gets
   manifests, hashes, metrics and text only.

## Consequences

- `gen_scale` drops from 16 to 4: 184 px cells become 736 px model canvases, inside the 512–768 px range the
  models were validated at.
- Track D bakes run cycles for the 5 generated directions, from a side/eye-level camera. Track C's pose templates
  come from that bake.
- Track A/B results compare speed and quality against a format the game can't use. Their "usable" flag is
  expected to be "no".
- Phase 2+ text that says "walk_4dir" now means the profile's locomotion clip. Later phases update their own wording
  when they start; this ADR does not rewrite them.
- Supersedes the 32×48, 4-direction and walk assumptions in `docs/05` and `docs/07` for the Twos Are Up profile.
